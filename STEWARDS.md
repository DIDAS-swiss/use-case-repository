# Domain stewards

Domain stewards approve stage transitions beyond `candidate` for the entries of
their domain (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#lifecycle-stages)). In AL2 a
steward's authority comes from an institutional mandate. In AL3 it is held by a
recognised sector body or ecosystem orchestrator (pre-study §7.3).

Where no steward is listed, the catalog curator holds provisional stewardship.

| Domain / sector | Ambition level | Steward | Organisation | Since |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *all, until confirmed* | AL2, AL3 | catalog curator (provisional) | DIDAS | 2026-10-30 |

To propose a steward, open an issue with the domain, the organisation and the
basis of its mandate.
