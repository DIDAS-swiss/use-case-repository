# What it attests

## What it attests

One paragraph: the facts the credential proves about its subject, and what it
replaces today.

## Issuer and mandate

Who holds the authoritative data or the mandate to attest it, and on what basis.
For a `missing` credential, say who could plausibly issue it.

a sectoral repository, not here.

## Schema and semantic references

Where the credential is published on I14Y, name the dataset and link it, and
set `i14y_dataset` and `i14y_id` so its schema is federated into
`federation/i14y/<id>/`. Otherwise link eCH standards or sectoral schemas, or
write "Not published on I14Y (checked YYYY-MM-DD)".

## Supply status

Why the status is `available`, `emerging` or `missing`, with a source.
