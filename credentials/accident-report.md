# Accident report

## What it attests

That a road accident was recorded: when, where, which parties and vehicles were involved, and what happened.

## Issuer and mandate

The cantonal police record accidents they attend. For accidents without police involvement the parties usually complete a joint accident statement. Whether that can become a credential is open.
