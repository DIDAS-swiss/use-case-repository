# 2026-10-30: Initial structure and seed use cases

## Added

- Repository structure for collecting use cases and credential needs, aimed
  at becoming an early version of the use-case section of the Swiss Ecosystem
  Discovery Catalog proposed in the DPSS pre-study of June 2026
  ([VISION.md](../VISION.md))
- Intake funnel modelled on the DIDAS Glossary: issue forms for use-case
  candidates, credential need and supply signals, and changes; lifecycle stages
  from pre-study §8.10; roles from pre-study §7.2 ([GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md))
- Entry templates and authoring guide ([CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md))
- Two seed use cases from the pre-study's flow work, at stage `signal`:
  vehicle leasing and motor vehicle claims processing
- Eight credential entries those use cases rely on, and a gap board ranking the
  missing ones by the number of use cases they block
- Workflows to create the label set (run manually) and to sort credential
  signals into `credential-need` and `credential-supply`

## Notes for review

- Issuers, statuses and scores in the seed entries follow the pre-study's
  *illustrative* mockups and are marked as such. Each needs confirmation by a
  domain steward before the entry moves past `signal`
- Legal bases in the seed use cases are marked "to be confirmed"
