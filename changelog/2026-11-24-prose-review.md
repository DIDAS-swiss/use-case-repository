# 2026-11-24: Documentation prose

## Changed

- VISION.md, GOVERNANCE.md, CONTRIBUTING.md, README.md, intake/README.md and the
  body text of the Backlog, Shared dependencies, Working groups and Credentials
  pages follow the writing rules:
  - findings of the DPSS pre-study are attributed to it, not stated in the
    repository's voice;
  - marketing phrases ("where the pieces meet", "open opportunities") and
    emphasis used to sell a claim are removed;
  - long sentences are split, and the actor is named;
  - claims without support are removed or marked, for example "most drivers
    making a motor claim have not leased their car".
- The gap board no longer says it is ranked; it gives the number of use cases
  each credential blocks.
- No change: PROJECT.md, Anchors, swiyu registries, Use-case ids, Use cases.
