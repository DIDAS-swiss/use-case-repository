# 2026-11-16: Active and open working groups; horizontal groups

## Changed

- `_data/working-groups.csv` records each group's `kind` (`sector` or
  `horizontal`) and `status` (`active` or `open`).
  - Active: the sector groups Mobility, Banking, Education, Health and Public
    Sector (eGov), and the horizontal group Technology.
  - Open, that is planned but not set up yet: Governance & Standards and
    Adoption (horizontal), and the other sector groups.
  - Open sector groups keep their sectors, so they are mapped when they start.
- The Working groups page lists active groups first, marks open ones, and shows
  the horizontal groups in their own table. The backlog filter marks open
  groups.
- The intake suggests only active groups. For a sector whose group is open, the
  comment says so and names the planned group.

## Added

- Horizontal groups Technology (active), Governance & Standards (open) and
  Adoption (open), with their `wg:` labels after *Sync labels*.
